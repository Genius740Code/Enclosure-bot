## The patch (`botF-retaliate.js`, new file only)
Byte-parallel copy of `bot-tourney.js`; ONLY the valuation changed. Widths (K1=10/K2=25/shortlist 25), search budgets (`BOT.searchBudget`), adaptive clock, and fallback chain identical. Three changes, exactly per the P0 prescription:
- **(a) quickScore break term**: `enemyEdgesRemoved * max(1.5, enemyAreaDelta * turnsLeft/40)` where `enemyAreaDelta = state.areas[opp] - result.areas[opp]` (the banked loop area that collapses the moment the break lands — engine recomputes `areas` every move) and `turnsLeft = lineLimit - moveNumber` (remaining payout horizon; enemy banks area at every turn end). bot-tourney valued breaks flat 1.5 → never retaliated against a banking loop (P0-1..P0-4).
- **(b) opponent-denial weight 0.15 → 0.35** at both valuation sites (the 1-action-terminal branch and the 2-ply branch share this knob). Engine fact that makes this real: scores only update at turn end, so the opp-delta term equals the enemy's banked area at our turn end — breaking reduces it by the popped loop area, i.e. (b) is a break reward of `0.35 × loopArea` in the final selection, on top of (a) in the search ranking.
- **(c) contact-avoidance**: a FIRST action whose new endpoint lands within 1.5 of an enemy node is penalized −1.0 unless it breaks something (`enemyEdgesRemoved === 0` gate); second-ply ranking not penalized (spec: first actions). Engine fact: an edge that geometrically touches an enemy node always breaks one of that node's segments (or is illegal — invincible / at-most-one-cut), so the only non-breaking contact is adjacency — exactly what neck/aggro graze.
